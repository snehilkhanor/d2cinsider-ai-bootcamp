# Catch-up kit, for founders who did not do the pre-work

Missed the setup session too? This page is for you.

You are not locked out of the day. Find your situation below and do only that part. Use **Raise hand** on Zoom at any point and a TA will take you into a breakout room.

The welcome and keynote run from 9:00 to 9:40. That is your window. Session 01 starts at 9:40.

---

## Situation 1: the app is not installed, or the folder is not open

Do steps 1 to 5 of [`session-00-setup.md`](session-00-setup.md) now. It takes about 15 minutes.

You are ready when you type `/` in the Code tab and see a list of teammates.

**If the install will not work at all** (a locked work laptop, no paid Claude plan, a tablet), tell a TA straight away. Do not spend the morning fighting it. You will follow the day on the instructor's screen, and you get the recordings and this folder to run every session at home.

## Situation 2: the app works, but `brand-brain/` is empty

This is the common one. Three pastes, about ten minutes.

**Paste 1, your website.** Replace the address with yours:

```
Copy templates/brand-brain to brand-brain/ if it does not exist yet. Then build a first draft of my brand-brain from my website: https://your-brand.com. Read the home page, the about page and my top three product pages. Fill positioning.md, products.csv and competitors.md with what you find. Mark every line you guessed with [CONFIRM]. Then ask me five questions, one at a time, to fix the most important guesses.
```

**Paste 2, your customers.** Open your reviews wherever they live: your Amazon listing, Google, Instagram comments, WhatsApp. Copy twenty or more, including the bad ones, and paste them into the chat with this line on top:

```
Save everything below into brand-brain/reviews.md exactly as written. Do not tidy or summarise it.
```

**Paste 3, your voice.** Find three things you wrote yourself and already sent: an Instagram caption, a WhatsApp reply to a customer, an email. Paste them with this line on top:

```
Save each of the pieces below as its own file in brand-brain/voice-dna/. Do not edit a word.
```

That is enough to run the whole day on your own brand. Session 01 interviews you for anything still missing.

## Situation 3: you have nothing to paste

No website yet, no reviews, or you are on a phone hotspot with no access to your files. Borrow the sample brand for today:

```
Copy examples/little-lab/brand-brain to brand-brain/, keeping every file.
```

You will run every session on Little Lab, a fictional baby skincare brand, and learn every step. **The output will not be about your brand.** At home, delete `brand-brain/` and `my-work/`, fill the template with your own material, and run the day again from Session 01. The second run is fast because you already know the moves.

---

## What you are missing, honestly

A founder who filled `brand-brain/` at home gets reports built on months of real reviews and real numbers. With the ten-minute version you get reports built on twenty reviews and a website. The steps are identical and the output is thinner.
