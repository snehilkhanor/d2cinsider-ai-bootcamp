# Sample Inputs, Little Lab

Raw exports of the kind a founder downloads from a store admin, an ads account and a support inbox. Invented, like everything else in this folder.

Use them when your own export is not ready, so you never sit out an exercise.

## For the Customer Researcher, Session 03

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `shopify-products.csv` | 9 products with price, stock, units sold, conversion rate, rating and review count |
| `reviews-export.csv` | 18 reviews from Amazon, Flipkart and the site, 15 April to 11 May 2026 |
| `support-tickets.csv` | 13 support tickets from email and WhatsApp |
| `gmail-support-threads.md` | 3 longer support conversations |

## For the Growth Analyst, Session 09

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `shopify-orders-28d.csv` | Daily orders by channel, 15 April to 12 May 2026 |
| `meta-ads-spend.csv` | Daily Meta spend by campaign and angle. The `hero-sku-lifestyle` angle starts on 5 May and is the one the brief flags |
| `google-ads-spend.csv` | Daily Google spend, two campaigns |

The last week's totals reconcile to `my-work/growth-analyst/2026-05-12-brief.md`.

## How to use one

Type `@` in the prompt box and pick the file, or drag it into the chat. For example:

```
Run /voice-of-customer on @examples/little-lab/sample-inputs/reviews-export.csv and @examples/little-lab/sample-inputs/support-tickets.csv
```
