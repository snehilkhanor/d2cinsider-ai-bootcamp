# Little Lab, worked example across all ten teammates

A full run of the D2C Insider AI Bootcamp on a fictional brand: **Little Lab**, baby skincare, about ₹4.2Cr a year, sold mostly on Amazon and Flipkart.

**The brand, the founder, the competitors, the retailers, the hospital and every number here are invented.** They are built to feel real. Do not quote any of it in a real deck. The compliance rules shown are illustrative and are not regulatory advice.

## Two uses

1. **To see what good looks like.** Before or after you run a teammate, open the matching folder here.
2. **To catch up.** If you fall behind, or did no pre-work, you can borrow this brand's files. See [`catch-up.md`](../../catch-up.md).

## Which folder goes with which session

| Session | Files |
|---|---|
| 01 Brand Strategist | `CLAUDE.md`, built from `brand-brain/` |
| 02 and 03 Competitive Analyst, Customer Researcher | `my-work/market-analyst/`, `my-work/voice-of-customer/` |
| 04 Content Director | `my-work/content-lead/`: calendar, five pieces, two marketplace listings, index |
| 05 Ad Creative Lead | `my-work/performance-marketer/`: two angles, creative briefs, index |
| 06 Marketplace Manager | `my-work/storefront-specialist/`: one product page before, gap and after, CRO notes. `my-work/marketplace-editor/` |
| 07 and 08 Ops Lead, Support and Retention Manager | `my-work/ops-manager/`: two SOPs, templates. `my-work/retention-manager/`: five WhatsApp, three emails, two segments |
| 09 Growth Analyst | `my-work/growth-analyst/`: brief, numbers, and `dashboard.html`, which opens with a double-click |
| 10 Chief of Staff | `my-work/captain/`: summary and 90 day roadmap |
| After the bootcamp | `my-work/influencer-scout/` |

## Good to know

- Everything here is Default scope, the size that fits a live session on a Pro plan.
- Dates are fixed, 12 to 19 May 2026, so the files read as one real run.
- Every teammate folder has an index. The Chief of Staff reads only those.

## Borrowing a folder when you are behind

Paste this into the app, changing the folder name to the teammate you need:

```
Copy examples/little-lab/my-work/content-lead into my-work/, keeping every file.
```

The next session now has something to read. It will be about Little Lab, not your brand, so re-run that teammate on your own brand at home.
