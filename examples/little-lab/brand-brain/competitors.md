# Competitors, Little Lab

One line per competitor explaining why they matter. Competitive Analyst in Session 2 expands these with real intel; this is the starting set.

- **Tender Root** (tenderroot.example): Direct D2C competitor, ayurvedic positioning, ₹130 to ₹530 price band, established Tier-1 and Tier-2 retail presence; beats us on shelf awareness and ayurvedic signal in Tier-2; we beat them by naming the active and printing the percentage where they stop at "ayurvedic blend".
- **First Nest** (firstnest.example): Direct competitor, "toxin-free" framing, Meridian Consumer-backed, premium spend; beats us on distribution depth and ad scale; we beat them by publishing lab reports for the specific compounds instead of relying on a marketing word the regulator does not recognise.
- **Bubloo** (bubloo.example): Adjacent broader baby D2C with app + community moat, content engine; beats us on engagement breadth across food, gear, skincare; we beat them on category focus, the parent shopping for cradle cap does not open the Bubloo app to solve it.
- **Leaf & Lull (Greenfold)** (leafandlull.example): Whole-family natural brand, listed company, dominant share of mind; beats us on awareness, ad spend and retail shelf; we beat them on ingredient transparency, our wedge converts the ex-Leaf & Lull buyer who finally read the label.
- **Pip & Poppy** (rforrabbit.com): Adjacent baby gear + skincare hybrid, retail-strong, gifting volume; beats us on cross-sell breadth and store distribution; we beat them on focus, we do not sell strollers and we never will, every SKU is paediatrically reviewed.
