# Template: reply to a melted or soft box

> I am so sorry. That is on us, not the courier. We should not have sent laddoos into that heat.
>
> Could you send me one photo? You do not need to return anything.
>
> A fresh box leaves our kitchen tomorrow with a cold pack and reaches you in two days. If you would rather have chikki or khajur bites, which travel better, tell me and I will send those.
>
> Meera

**Rules for whoever sends this:** do not add an offer or a code. Do not say "inconvenience". If it was a gift, add: "Was this for a particular day? Tell me and I will see what I can do."
