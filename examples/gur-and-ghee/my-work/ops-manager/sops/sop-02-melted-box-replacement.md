# SOP 02: A box arrived soft, oily or melted

**Owner:** whoever is on customer messages that day.
**Why this exists:** customer voice report, theme 5. `brand-brain/voice-dna/whatsapp-replies.md` shows how Meera already handles it.

## Within 2 hours of the message

1. Reply using `templates/melted-box-reply.md`. First sentence says it was us.
2. Ask for one photo. Do not ask for the box back. Do not ask for an explanation.

## Same day

3. Check the destination against Monday's paused list.
   - **City was paused and we shipped anyway:** log it as a dispatch error, tell Meera today.
   - **City was not paused:** log the temperature that day. Three of these in one city moves it onto the list.
4. Create a replacement order at ₹0 with liner and gel pack, two day service.
5. If the city is now paused, offer chikki or khajur bites now, or laddoos when the pause lifts. The customer chooses.

## If it was a gift

6. Ask whether the gift had a date. If the date has passed, Meera calls the customer herself.

## Log

Date, order number, city, forecast high, product, photo received, replacement sent, cost.

## Escalate to Meera when

- It was a gift and the date was missed.
- The customer is a corporate buyer.
- It is the second problem on the same customer.
