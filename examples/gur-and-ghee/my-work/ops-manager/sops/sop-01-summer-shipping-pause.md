# SOP 01: Summer shipping pause for ghee laddoos

**Owner:** kitchen dispatch lead. **Approver:** Meera.
**Why this exists:** customer voice report, theme 5. Three melted boxes in May, 4.1% replacement rate against 1.2% in January.

## The rule

A ghee laddoo order ships only if the forecast daytime high at the destination is **40°C or below on every day of the expected transit**.

## Every Monday, 10 am

1. Open the weather forecast for the 30 cities on the dispatch sheet.
2. Mark any city forecast above 40°C on any day this week as **Paused**.
3. Send the paused list to whoever manages the site by 11 am.
4. On the site, paused pin codes see "Back when it is cooler" on laddoo pages, with the chikki shown below.

## For an order to a city at 36°C to 40°C

1. Insulated liner and one gel pack. Adds ₹38.
2. Dispatch Monday to Wednesday only, so it does not sit in a depot over a weekend.
3. Two day service only.

## What never pauses

Peanut Gur Chikki, Gur Makhana, Khajur Gur Bites. They ship everywhere, all year.

## Exceptions

Only Meera approves one. The customer is told the risk in writing first.

## Review

First Monday of every month from April to July. Check the replacement rate. If it is above 2%, lower the threshold by two degrees.
