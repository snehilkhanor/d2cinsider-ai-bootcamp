# Instagram carousel: Gur is sugar

**Goes live:** Mon 15 June
**Source:** customer voice report, theme 6. Three customers asked if the laddoos are safe for a diabetic parent.

**Slide 1**
Gur is sugar.

**Slide 2**
Our boxes say "no refined sugar, sweetened with jaggery".
Some of you have read that as "no sugar".
That is our wording, and we are fixing it.

**Slide 3**
What is true:
We use gur instead of white sugar.
We use ghee instead of vanaspati.
Three ingredients in the til laddoo. Nothing else.

**Slide 4**
What is also true:
Gur raises blood sugar.
If someone you love is managing diabetes, please ask their doctor before sending them our laddoos.

**Slide 5**
We would rather lose the order.

**Caption**
Three of you asked us this month whether our laddoos are okay for a diabetic parent. The honest answer is on these slides. If you have already sent a box to someone and you are worried, message us and Meera will reply.
