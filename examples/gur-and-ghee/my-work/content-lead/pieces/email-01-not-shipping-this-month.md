# Email: Why your laddoos are not shipping this month

**Goes out:** Wed 17 June, full list
**Source:** customer voice report, theme 5. `brand-brain/voice-dna/founder-note-on-summer.md`

**Subject:** Why I will not send you laddoos this month

Hi,

Meera here.

In May we sent ghee laddoos to cities where it was 41 degrees. One in twenty-five boxes arrived soft or leaking. One customer in Nagpur asked, fairly, why we ship this in May at all.

So until 30 June, we do not. If your city is above 40 in the daytime, the laddoo pages will show "back on 1 July" and will not let you order.

What does ship, everywhere, all summer:

- Peanut Gur Chikki
- Gur Makhana
- Khajur Gur Bites

None of them have enough ghee to mind the heat.

If you need laddoos before July for a reason that matters, reply to this email. If I can get a box to you in two days with a cold pack, I will. If I cannot, I will say so.

Meera
