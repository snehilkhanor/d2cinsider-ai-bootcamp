# Blog: We do not discount. This is the reason

**Goes live:** Mon 22 June
**Source:** competitor report, executive read point 1. `CLAUDE.md` Section 3.

We have never run a sale. Not at Diwali, not on our birthday, not when you leave something in your cart.

This is not a strategy. It is arithmetic.

A box of Til Gur Laddoo costs us about ₹160 to make and ₹150 to pack and send. We sell it for ₹449. If we took 15% off in October, we would have to get that ₹67 back from somewhere. There are only three places: the sesame, the gur or the ghee.

Most sweet brands run a sale before every festival. I understand why. But it means the price in September was never the real price, and I do not know how to look a customer in the eye over that.

So the price is the price. In June and in October. For one box and for three hundred.

What we do instead:

- Order five boxes or more and shipping is on us.
- Every gift box has a card, written by hand, with whatever you ask us to write.
- If a box arrives in a state you would not gift, we send another. You do not send anything back.

That is the whole policy.
