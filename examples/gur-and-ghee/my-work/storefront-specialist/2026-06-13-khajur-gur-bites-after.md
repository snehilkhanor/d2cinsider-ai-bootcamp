# Khajur Gur Bites, after

**Status:** draft. Not published.
**Source:** customer voice report, theme 3 and the tiffin parent persona. Winners pattern.

## Khajur Gur Bites, pack of 16

Dates, gur, cashew and a little ghee, rolled small enough for a tiffin box.

"My daughter thinks it's a toffee." That is a customer in Bengaluru, and we have not corrected her daughter.

They are a sweet. They have dates and gur in them, which are both sugar. What they do not have is a wrapper with a cartoon on it or an ingredient you cannot pronounce.

**What is in it:** dates (52%), jaggery (22%), cashew (18%), ghee (8%). Nothing else.

**How parents tell us they use it:** one in the tiffin. One after dinner, instead of chocolate. One for themselves, while packing the tiffin.

**Keeps for:** 60 days. Ships to every city, all year.

**Contains:** tree nuts (cashew), milk (ghee). Made in a kitchen that also handles sesame, peanuts and wheat.

₹549

## Hypothesis

Showing the child and the tiffin, in the customer's own words, gets the parent to try it once. The rating says they stay. **Expected:** conversion from 1.4% towards 2.0% in 14 days.

## Flags

- **"Instead of chocolate" is a customer's phrase, reported as what parents tell us.** It makes no nutritional comparison. Confirm you are comfortable with it.
- **I guessed the percentages.** Replace with the real recipe.
- **Two of four reviewers asked for a bigger pack.** That is a product decision, not a page decision.
