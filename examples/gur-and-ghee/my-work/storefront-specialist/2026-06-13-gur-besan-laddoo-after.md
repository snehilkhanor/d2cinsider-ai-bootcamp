## Gur Besan Laddoo, box of 12

Roasted besan, gur and ghee. Roasted slowly, for longer than is convenient, until it smells like a kitchen in December.

This is a firm, crumbly laddoo. It is the Rajasthani kind, made to be had with chai, and it will not melt in your mouth. If you grew up on the soft kind, the Dry Fruit Gur Laddoo is closer to what you are looking for.

**What is in it:** besan (46%), jaggery (34%), ghee (20%). Nothing else.

**Have it with:** chai. One customer told us it needed chai to get through. She is right, and that is the idea.

**Keeps for:** 21 days from the date on the box. Somewhere cool. In summer, the fridge.

**Contains:** milk (ghee). Made in a kitchen that also handles sesame, peanuts, tree nuts and wheat.

₹499
