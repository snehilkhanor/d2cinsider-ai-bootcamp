# CRO Observations, Gur & Ghee

**Date:** 2026-06-13. From `brand-brain/products.csv` and the pasted product pages. No analytics access, so these are observations, not measurements.

1. **The pages that list ingredients with percentages convert best.** Til Gur Laddoo, 3.4%. The two pages without a list are the two worst.
2. **No page answers the diabetic question.** Every one of those questions arrives on WhatsApp. Add one line under the ingredients on every laddoo page: "Gur is sugar. If you are buying for someone managing diabetes, please ask their doctor first."
3. **No page mentions delivery time.** Three customers complained about it. Even an honest "5 to 8 days outside metros" would help.
4. **Nothing on the site says "we never discount".** A customer waiting for a sale that will never come is a lost order.
5. **Corporate gifting is a paragraph and a phone number.** Two enquiries in May could not find a way in. This is the landing page to build next.
6. **During the shipping pause, a paused page should offer the chikki, not a dead end.**
