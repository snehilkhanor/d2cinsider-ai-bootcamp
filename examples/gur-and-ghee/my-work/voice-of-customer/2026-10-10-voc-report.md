# Customer Voice Report, Gur & Ghee

**Date:** 2026-10-10
**Source:** live store reviews, the support inbox and WhatsApp, connected in July. 148 messages, 1 August to 8 October 2026.
**Compared with:** `2026-06-13-voc-report.md`, 32 messages.

## Top read in 5 lines

1. **The diabetic question has almost stopped.** 3 of 32 in May (9%). 2 of 148 now (1%). The sugar line went on every laddoo page on 15 June.
2. **"Dry" has gone from the besan laddoo reviews.** 3 of 3 in May, 1 of 14 now. The recipe did not change. The page did.
3. **No melted box since 1 August.** The pause rule held through September.
4. **The new top complaint is corporate, and it is about us being slow:** 11 buyers waited more than a day for a reply in the last three weeks.
5. **11 customers asked for a bigger pack of Khajur Gur Bites.** In May it was 2.

## What is different about this report

The June report had 32 messages pasted by hand and could only say what people felt. This one has 148 from live sources and a report to compare against, so it can say **what changed after you acted**. Three of June's themes are nearly gone, and each one traces to a specific change made in the week after the bootcamp.
