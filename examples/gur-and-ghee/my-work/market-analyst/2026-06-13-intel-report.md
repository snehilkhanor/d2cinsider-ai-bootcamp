# Competitor Intel, Gur & Ghee

**Date:** 2026-06-13
**Set:** the four competitors in `brand-brain/competitors.md`. All four are fictional, written for this example.
**Method:** public pages only. Prices marked unverified could not be confirmed on the day.

## Executive read in 5 lines

1. **Everyone in the set discounts except you.** Three of four are running an offer today. Your no-discount rule is a real difference, and nothing on your site says it.
2. **Sweetroot is winning the search you would expect to win**, "healthier sweet", by making a claim you have ruled out. Do not chase it. Take the customers who have been let down by it.
3. **Nobody is selling "not too sweet".** It is your customers' own phrase (6 of 32 in the customer voice report) and it appears on no competitor homepage.
4. **Mithai Theory has a corporate gifting page with a form, a catalogue and a 24 hour reply promise.** You have a paragraph and a phone number. That gap is worth more than any ad.
5. **No competitor says anything about summer shipping.** Three of your customers received melted boxes. The first brand to be honest about heat owns that trust.

## Per competitor

### Mithai Theory

- **Position:** modern mithai as a design object. Mumbai.
- **Price:** gift boxes ₹899 to ₹2,400. Comparable 12 piece box ₹749.
- **Running now:** "Summer Edit, 15% off" banner. Mango range.
- **Content:** posts daily, mostly packaging and flat lays. Almost no kitchen, no founder.
- **Corporate:** dedicated page, PDF catalogue, enquiry form, "we reply in 24 hours", logo sleeve from 50 boxes.
- **Weak spot:** reviews praise the box and are lukewarm on taste. Uses refined sugar in most of the range.

### Sweetroot

- **Position:** "guilt-free" laddoos sweetened with dates. Bengaluru.
- **Price:** ₹599 for 12. Subscription at ₹539.
- **Running now:** "Diabetic friendly" on ad creative and in the page title. 10% off first subscription box.
- **Content:** nutritionist reels, comparison charts against regular mithai.
- **Weak spot:** the claim. Dates are sugar too. Reviews include customers asking why their readings went up. That is the customer who will trust a brand that says "gur is sugar".

### Halwai & Sons 1962

- **Position:** the Jaipur default. Sixty years, four shops.
- **Price:** ₹380 for 500g, roughly 40% below you by weight.
- **Running now:** nothing online. Swiggy listing with 4.4 from several thousand ratings.
- **Content:** none to speak of.
- **Weak spot:** refined sugar, plain boxes, does not ship outside Rajasthan. Not a threat nationally. In Jaipur on Zomato and Swiggy, they are who you are compared against, and price is the comparison.

### Desi Jar

- **Position:** jaggery snacks for the marketplace shopper.
- **Price:** peanut chikki ₹199 for 200g, against your ₹249. Price unverified, it changes weekly.
- **Running now:** "Buy 2 get 1". On Amazon, Blinkit and Zepto in eleven cities.
- **Content:** product shots only.
- **Weak spot:** palm oil in two products, long ingredient lists, no story. On Blinkit in Jaipur they sit directly above you.

## Patterns across the set

| | Mithai Theory | Sweetroot | Halwai & Sons | Desi Jar | Gur & Ghee |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Discounting today | Yes | Yes | No | Yes | **Never** |
| Health claim in copy | No | Yes | No | Mild | **Never** |
| Founder visible | No | Partly | Family name | No | **Yes** |
| Corporate page | Strong | None | None | None | **Weak** |
| Says anything about heat | No | No | No | No | **One founder note** |
| Ships nationally | Yes | Yes | No | Yes | Yes |

**The open ground:** honest about sugar, honest about heat, never on sale, and the founder answers. Nobody else in the set can say all four.

**The closed ground:** price in Jaipur, and reach on quick commerce. Do not fight either.

## Founder asks

1. Are you willing to say "we never discount" on the site, in those words? It is your strongest difference and it is invisible.
2. How many corporate enquiries did you get last October, and how many did you answer within a day?
3. Do you want to be on Blinkit at all? You sit under a cheaper chikki there, with no room to tell the story.
