# Weekly Brief, Gur & Ghee, 2026-10-10

**Week covered:** 3 to 9 October 2026
**Data:** live store and ad accounts, connected in July. Kitchen capacity from the dispatch sheet in Drive.

## Headline

The biggest week the brand has had: ₹22,14,000, of which ₹14,18,000 is corporate. The risk this week is not demand. It is that the kitchen fills up before your own customers have ordered for Diwali.

## The one alarm

**Kitchen capacity booked for 19 October to 6 November: 52% last week, 71% this week.**

31 corporate orders were confirmed in seven days. At this rate the kitchen is full around 17 October, three weeks before Diwali on 8 November. Corporate buyers pay in 30 to 45 days. Site customers pay today and are the ones who reorder in January.

## This week's action

**Announce a corporate closing date of 16 October, today.** Put it on the corporate page and send it to every open enquiry. Hold 20% of capacity for the site.

**Expected result:** corporate bookings stop at about 80% by 16 October. Site Diwali orders ship without a cut-off before 1 November.

## Top 3 reads

1. **You are filling the kitchen at full price.** Mithai Theory is offering 20% off corporate orders before 15 October. Source: competitor report, 10 October.
2. **Cost per new customer is ₹291 and has moved ₹23 in four weeks** while spend doubled. The two angles written in June are still the two best performing.
3. **Repeat rate is now measured, not assumed: 33%.** Senders with a saved address reorder at 41%.
