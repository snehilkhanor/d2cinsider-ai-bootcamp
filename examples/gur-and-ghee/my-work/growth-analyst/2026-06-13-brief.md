# Weekly Brief, Gur & Ghee, 2026-06-13

**Week covered:** 6 to 12 June 2026
**Data:** exported files in `sample-inputs/`, and `brand-brain/unit-economics.md`. No live connection.

## Headline

Revenue is up 14% on last week to ₹5,55,527, and almost all of the rise is four corporate orders. Underneath, the site is getting quieter each week and the cost of a new customer has nearly doubled in a month.

## The one alarm

**Blended cost per new customer: ₹293 last week, ₹346 this week, up 18%.** It was ₹192 four weeks ago. Your own number is ₹285, so this is now 21% above what you believe it to be.

Two causes:

1. **The `khajur-tiffin` campaign.** Live since 1 June. It spent ₹17,144 this week and brought 21 customers, ₹816 each. Your other two campaigns cost ₹342 and ₹356.
2. **Fewer people can buy.** Laddoos have been paused in hot cities since 25 May. New site customers fell from 216 to 198, while ad spend rose from ₹63,216 to ₹68,557.

## This week's action

**Pause `khajur-tiffin` today.** Do not move its budget to the other campaigns until 1 July, when laddoos ship everywhere again. Relaunch Khajur Gur Bites after the new product page is published, using a tiffin angle that names a child.

**Expected result:** blended cost per customer back under ₹300 next week. If it is not, the pause is the bigger cause and spend should come down further until July.

## Top 3 reads

1. **The product is fine, the ad is wrong.** Khajur Gur Bites is your best rated product (4.75). Both ads you have run for it lead with a benefit word. Source: winners pattern.
2. **Site orders have fallen four weeks running:** 462, 403, 379, 354. That is the shipping pause, and it is the right call. But you are paying for traffic that lands on a page it cannot buy from.
3. **Corporate was 29% of this week's revenue from 4 orders.** It arrives with no ad spend. Two more enquiries in May could not find a way to order.
