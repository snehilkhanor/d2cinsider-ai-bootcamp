# Winners Pattern, Gur & Ghee

**Date:** 2026-06-13
**Source:** `brand-brain/ads/ad-copy-bank.md`, four ads with founder notes. `sample-inputs/meta-ads-spend.csv`, three live campaigns.

## The pattern

**Your ads work when there is a specific person in the first line and no benefit word anywhere.** Nani. Papa. Your parents. The two ads that open with a benefit are your two worst, and both are for the same product.

The Khajur Gur Bites problem is the ad, not the product. It has the best rating in your range (4.75) and both ads for it describe a category instead of a child.

## What this means for new angles

- Open with a person or a moment. The customer voice report gives you two ready-made: the phone call from a parent, and the child who thinks it is a toffee.
- Name what is in it. Stop there.
- Never use a benefit adjective. It is forbidden by the claim wall and it also does not work.

## Confidence

Six ads is a small set, and two of them have no clean numbers. The pattern is consistent across all six, which is why I am reporting it. Treat it as a strong hunch to test.
