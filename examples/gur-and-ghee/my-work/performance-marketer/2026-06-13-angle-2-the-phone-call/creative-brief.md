# Creative brief, angle 2: the phone call

**Format:** single image, 4:5, for Meta feed
**Product:** Festive Gift Box

## The one idea

The box has just been opened by someone older. We see their hands and the card, not their face.

## Shot

Slightly above eye level. An open box on a plain cotton tablecloth. An older man's hands, one holding a handwritten card, reading glasses folded on the table. A steel tumbler of chai. Afternoon light.

## Text on image

None. The card is legible enough to read "Papa,".

## Do not

No faces. No festive decoration, this runs all year. No phone in frame. No staged smiling family.

## Image prompt

Photograph from slightly above of an open cardboard sweet box holding round sesame laddoos on a plain cream cotton tablecloth, an older Indian man's hands holding a small handwritten card, folded reading glasses and a steel tumbler of tea beside the box, soft afternoon window light, natural colours, quiet domestic feeling, no faces visible, no text, 4:5

**Negative prompt:** face, smiling family, festive lights, diyas, phone, logo, text overlay, glossy, stock photo look
