# Creative brief, angle 1: not too sweet

**Format:** single image, 4:5, for Meta feed
**Product:** Peanut Gur Chikki 200g

## The one idea

A broken piece of chikki, close enough to see the peanuts. Nothing else in frame that is not in the recipe.

## Shot

Top-down. One slab of chikki snapped in two on a dark steel thali. A few loose roasted peanuts, a small lump of gur, a steel spoon with ghee. Late morning window light from the left, hard enough to throw a shadow.

## Text on image

"Not too sweet. On purpose." Bottom left, small.

## Do not

No hands with rings and mehndi. No marigolds. No wooden boards. No steam added afterwards. No "healthy" props such as measuring tapes or green leaves.

## Image prompt

Top-down photograph of a slab of peanut jaggery chikki snapped in two on a dark brushed steel plate, visible whole roasted peanuts in amber jaggery, a few loose peanuts, a small rough lump of dark jaggery and a steel spoon of ghee beside it, hard late-morning window light from the left, deep natural shadows, matte finish, muted warm colours, shallow depth, no text, 4:5

**Negative prompt:** text, logo, hands, flowers, wooden board, steam, glossy, oversaturated, studio softbox look
