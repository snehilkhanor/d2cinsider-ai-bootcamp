# Email: day 10, how was it

**Trigger:** 10 days after delivery. **Source:** customer voice report, themes 1 and 2.

**Subject:** One question

Hi {{first_name}},

Your box has been with you about ten days, which means it is probably empty.

One question. Who ate most of it?

Reply with one line. I read all of these, and the answers are how we decide what to make next.

Meera

**Why it asks this:** the reply tells us whether the buyer is a sender, a tiffin parent or eating it themselves. That sets the segment.
