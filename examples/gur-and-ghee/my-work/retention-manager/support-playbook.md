## 1. Diabetes and sugar

> Hi, no, they are not sugar free. We use gur instead of refined sugar, but gur is still sugar. If someone is managing diabetes, please check with their doctor before they have these. I would rather lose the order than tell you otherwise.

Never suggest a product as the "better option". Never say low sugar, less sugar or safer.

## 2. Melted box

Use `my-work/ops-manager/templates/melted-box-reply.md`. Follow SOP 02.

## 3. Late or stuck

> I am sorry, that is slower than it should be. I am checking with the courier now and will message you by 5 pm today with where it is and when it will reach you.

Then send `my-work/ops-manager/vendor-kit/courier-escalation.md`. Message the customer by 5 pm even if there is no news.

## 4. Dry besan laddoo

> Thank you for telling me. Our besan laddoo is the firm, crumbly Rajasthani kind, made to go with chai, and our page did not say that clearly. That is our mistake. If it is not what you were hoping for, I will send you the Dry Fruit Gur Laddoo instead, on us.

## 5. Small for the price

> That is fair, they are small. We make them by hand in batches of forty with gur and ghee, and that is what it costs us to do it properly. We would rather make it this size than change what goes in.

Do not apologise for the price. Do not offer anything.

## 6. Corporate and bulk

> Yes, we do, and yes, you get a GST invoice. Minimum 25 boxes, your logo on the sleeve from 50. Could you tell me how many boxes, the date, and the city? Meera will reply to you herself within one working day.

Pass to Meera immediately.

## 7. Discount

> We do not do discounts, on anything, ever. What I can do for five boxes or more is free shipping and a handwritten card in each one. Shall I set that up?

## 8. Gajak

> November, when it is cold enough to make it properly. I will message you the day it is.

Add them to the gajak list.
