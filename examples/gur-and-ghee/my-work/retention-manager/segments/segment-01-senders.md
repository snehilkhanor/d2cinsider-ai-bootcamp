# Segment 01: senders

**Definition:** any customer with at least one order where the delivery pin code differs from the billing pin code, or who chose the handwritten card.

**Estimated size:** not known. Needs an order export with both addresses.

**Why they matter:** customer voice report, theme 2. They buy the highest value boxes and they buy on dates: birthdays, anniversaries, festivals.

**What they get:** WhatsApp 02 at day 35. A reminder two weeks before Raksha Bandhan and Diwali with the saved address.

**What they never get:** more than one message a month.
