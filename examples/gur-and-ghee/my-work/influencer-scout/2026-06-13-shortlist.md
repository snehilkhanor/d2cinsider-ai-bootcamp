## Ruled out

- Fitness and diet creators. Any post from them implies a health claim.
- Anyone who has posted "diabetic friendly" sweets.
- Creators who only post discount codes. You have no code to give.

## Suggested first step

Send a box to 2 and 5 with a handwritten card and no brief. Both are more likely to post something true than something paid.
