# 90 Day Operating Roadmap, Gur & Ghee

**Written:** 2026-06-13. **Covers:** 15 June to 12 September 2026.

## The brand in one paragraph

Gur & Ghee makes nine jaggery-sweetened sweets in Jaipur and does about ₹22L a month, rising to ₹64L in Diwali month. It never discounts and never makes a health claim. Its customers love it for being "not too sweet" and for what their parents say when a box arrives. Its three live problems are a pack line that diabetics' families misread, a summer in which its hero product cannot ship to half the country, and a corporate gifting business worth a fifth of revenue with no front door.

## The state of the chain right now

Nine teammates ran today on 32 customer messages and 28 days of exported orders. Everything is a first pass on thin data. The competitor set and the customer themes are solid enough to act on. The numbers on repeat rate and margin by channel are the founder's own and unchecked.

- Launch angle 1, "not too sweet", on the chikki. Three variants, ₹1,500 a day.

- Settle the three guessed support policies and write them into `CLAUDE.md`.

## Week 3, 29 June to 5 July

- Shipping pause lifts 1 July. Send WhatsApp template 05 and the "shipping again" email.
- Launch angle 2, "the phone call", on the Festive Gift Box.
- Relaunch Khajur Gur Bites ads on a tiffin angle. Target under ₹350 a customer.

## Week 4, 6 to 12 July

- Corporate gifting page live. LinkedIn post to HR teams.
- Load WhatsApp template 02, send it home again.
- Re-run the Customer Researcher on June and July reviews. Check whether the diabetic question has stopped.

## Month 2, 13 July to 12 August: three priorities

1. **Corporate, before the season.** Orders open 1 August. Write the corporate SOP. Decide capacity: how many boxes a week the kitchen can make, and how much of it corporate may take.
2. **Connect the store and ad accounts.** See `after-the-bootcamp/connectors.md`. The Growth Analyst is estimating three numbers it should be measuring.
3. **Decide on Blinkit and Amazon.** Both put you beside a cheaper chikki with no room for the story. The listing draft exists so the answer is informed.

## Month 3, 13 August to 12 September: three priorities

1. **New pack wording at the next print run.** "Sweetened with gur. Gur is sugar."
2. **Diwali content and ads, built on what worked in July.** No offers. Lead with the card and the parent.
3. **A 24 piece Khajur Gur Bites.** Two of four reviewers asked. Test it for one month.

## What this roadmap is not

It is not a forecast. It does not know your cash position, your kitchen's real capacity or what your team can absorb. Cut it where it is too much.

## What I did not synthesise

- **Influencer Scout.** Has a shortlist. I did not weigh it against ad spend because there is no creator budget in `brand-brain/`.
- **Cash.** Corporate pays 30 to 45 days late and Diwali needs ingredients bought in September. Nothing in my inputs covers this, and it may be the most important number of the quarter.
