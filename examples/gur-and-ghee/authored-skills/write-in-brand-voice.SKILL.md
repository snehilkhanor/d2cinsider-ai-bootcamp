# Write in the Gur & Ghee voice

## Before writing anything

1. Read `CLAUDE.md` Section 7, the voice rules and the claim wall.
2. Read three to five files from `brand-brain/voice-dna/` that match the format being asked for. A WhatsApp reply reads `whatsapp-replies.md`. A caption reads the Instagram file.

## Rules I added by hand

- Name the ingredient, then stop. "Til, gur, ghee." Never "a symphony of".
- Sentences are short. If a sentence has two commas, split it.
- One dry line is allowed per piece. Two is trying too hard.
- Say gur, til, nani. Do not italicise or translate them.
- If something went wrong, say it was us in the first sentence.
- Never offer a discount. Offer free shipping or a handwritten card instead.
- Never write sugar free, diabetic friendly, healthy, guilt-free or immunity. If the reader asked about diabetes, say plainly that gur is sugar and they should ask their doctor.

## After writing

Read it out loud as a WhatsApp message. If I would not send it, rewrite it.
