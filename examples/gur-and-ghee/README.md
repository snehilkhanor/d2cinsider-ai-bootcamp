# Gur & Ghee, worked example across all ten teammates

A full run of the D2C Insider AI Bootcamp on a fictional brand: **Gur & Ghee**, jaggery-sweetened laddoos and chikki made in Jaipur, sold on its own site, on Zomato and Swiggy in its home city, and to corporate gifting buyers.

**The brand, the founder, the competitors and every number here are invented.** They are built to feel real. Do not quote any of it in a real deck.

## Why this brand is in the repo

It is the opposite shape to Little Lab. Little Lab sells a trust product through marketplaces under cosmetics rules. Gur & Ghee sells a perishable food through its own site under FSSAI rules, with a summer shipping problem and a gifting season that makes or breaks the year.

Three things are worth looking for:

1. **A claim the customer misreads.** The packs say "no refined sugar". Three customers in the review set read that as "safe for diabetics". Watch how that one finding travels from the customer voice report into the claim wall, the product pages, the ads and the support playbook.
2. **A rule that costs money on purpose.** The brand never discounts. See how the retention flows win customers back without an offer.
3. **Two dated runs.** The competitor report, customer voice report and growth brief each exist for June 2026 and October 2026. Same brand, four months apart. The October ones are sharper because the inputs got better.
