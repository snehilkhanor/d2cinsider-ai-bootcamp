# Til Gur Laddoo, box of 12

Roasted white sesame, Kolhapuri gur, a spoon of ghee. Rolled by hand while the gur is still warm, which is why no two are exactly round.

They are sweet. Gur is sugar, we are not going to pretend otherwise. But it is a slower, darker sweetness and you will taste the til first.

**What is in it:** sesame (58%), jaggery (38%), ghee (4%). Nothing else.

**Keeps for:** 21 days from the date on the box. Keep it somewhere cool. In summer, the fridge.

**Contains:** sesame, milk (ghee). Made in a kitchen that also handles peanuts, tree nuts and wheat.
