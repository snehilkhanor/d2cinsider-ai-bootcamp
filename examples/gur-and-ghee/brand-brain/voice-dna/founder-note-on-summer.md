A note about summer.

From today, 25 May, to 30 June we are not shipping ghee laddoos to cities where the daytime temperature is above 40. This year that list is long.

This month we shipped anyway. One in twenty-five boxes arrived soft or leaking, and each of those was somebody's gift.

Chikki, makhana and khajur bites travel fine and ship everywhere, all year.

If your city is on the pause list and you want laddoos, message me. If I can get it to you in two days with a cold pack, I will. If I cannot, I will tell you.

Meera
