# Competitors, Gur & Ghee

All four are fictional, written for this example.

| Competitor | Who they are | Price point | What they do well | Where they are weak |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mithai Theory** | Mumbai, premium "modern mithai", design-led | Boxes ₹899 to ₹2,400 | Packaging, corporate gifting desk, Instagram | Runs 15% off before every festival, tastes like a brand deck |
| **Sweetroot** | Bengaluru, date-sweetened "guilt-free" laddoos | ₹599 for 12 | Subscription, strong repeat, clear single promise | Says "diabetic friendly" on ads, which we will not and cannot say |
| **Halwai & Sons 1962** | Jaipur legacy sweet shop, now on Swiggy and its own site | ₹380 for 500g | Trust in Jaipur, price, range | Refined sugar, plain boxes, does not ship beyond Rajasthan |
| **Desi Jar** | Marketplace-heavy jaggery snacks and chikki | Chikki ₹199 for 200g | Amazon and quick commerce reach, always in stock | Discount-led, palm oil in some SKUs, no founder, no story |

**Who I actually lose orders to:** Mithai Theory for corporate gifting, Halwai & Sons for walk-in Jaipur customers, Sweetroot for the "healthier sweet" search.
