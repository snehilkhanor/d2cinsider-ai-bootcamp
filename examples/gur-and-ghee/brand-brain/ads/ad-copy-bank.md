# Ad Copy Bank, Gur & Ghee

What actually ran, with what I know about how it did.

## Meta, Til Gur Laddoo, "nani's recipe" (live Jan to Mar 2026)

> My nani made these every Sankranti. Til, gur, ghee. That's the list. I make them the same way, just in a cleaner kitchen. Box of 12, ₹449.

Best ad I have run. CTR 2.3%, CAC about ₹260. Stopped it in April because of summer shipping.

## Meta, Festive Gift Box, "send it home" (live Oct to Nov 2025)

> You can't be home this Diwali. The box can. 24 pieces, a card in your handwriting, delivered to your parents' door.

Worked very well for three weeks, CAC around ₹300, then cost doubled the week before Diwali when everyone else arrived.

## Meta, Khajur Gur Bites, "healthy treat for kids" (ran 2 weeks, Feb 2026, stopped)

> A healthy treat your kids will actually eat.

Did nothing. CTR 0.6%. I also should not have said healthy. My food consultant told me off.

## Google Search, "gur laddoo online" (live all year)

> Til Gur Laddoo, Made in Small Batches | No Refined Sugar | Ships Across India

Steady. About 40 orders a month at ₹210 CAC. Small volume.

## What is missing

No performance data by product. Nothing ever run for corporate gifting. Nothing run on Zomato or Swiggy.

## What we will not run

No discount ads. No "sugar free", "diabetic friendly", "healthy", "guilt-free" or "immunity". No before and after. No comparison naming another brand.
