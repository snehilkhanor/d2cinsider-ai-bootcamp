# Unit Economics, Gur & Ghee (90-day view, to end May 2026)

My numbers, from my own sheet. Rough where marked.

## Headline

About ₹22L a month outside festival season. Diwali month last year was ₹64L, and almost half of that was corporate. Profitable on contribution, thin after my own salary.

## AOV

- Own site: ₹742
- Zomato and Swiggy: ₹410
- Corporate: ₹38,000 per order, lumpy, 9 to 12 orders in a normal month

## Gross margin (own site, per order)

| Line | ₹ | % of AOV |
|---|---|---|
| AOV | 742 | 100% |
| Ingredients and making | 267 | 36% |
| Box, liner, card | 62 | 8.4% |
| Shipping | 88 | 11.9% |
| Payment gateway | 15 | 2% |
| **Contribution before marketing** | **310** | **41.8%** |

April to June I add an insulated liner and a gel pack: ₹38 more per order. That takes contribution to ₹272.

## CAC

- Blended, new customers: ₹285
- Meta only: ₹340
- Zomato and Swiggy: I pay 24% commission instead, no separate CAC

## LTV

Rough. A site customer orders 1.9 times in 12 months. ₹742 x 1.9 = ₹1,410 revenue, about ₹590 contribution. I have never done a proper cohort pull.

## Channel mix (90-day, % of revenue)

| Channel | Share |
|---|---|
| Own site | 55% |
| Corporate gifting | 20% |
| Zomato and Swiggy, Jaipur | 17% |
| Blinkit, Jaipur trial | 8% |

## Repeat purchase rate

29% of site customers order again within 90 days. Higher for Til Gur Laddoo buyers, I think, but I have not checked.

## Notes and uncertainties

- Replacements for melted or soft orders: 4.1% of orders in May, 1.2% in January. Each one costs me the order twice.
- Corporate margin is better than site margin, about 48%, but payment comes 30 to 45 days late.
- I do not know my CAC by product.
- Blinkit numbers are from their dashboard and I do not fully trust them.
