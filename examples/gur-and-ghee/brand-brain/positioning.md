# Positioning, Gur & Ghee

We make the sweets my nani made, with gur instead of refined sugar and ghee instead of vanaspati, in batches small enough that I can taste every one. We are for people who want to send or serve a proper Indian sweet and not apologise for it. We are not a health food, we are not a diet product, and we are not a mithai shop with four hundred items. Nine products, made well.
