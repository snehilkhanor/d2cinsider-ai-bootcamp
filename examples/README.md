# Examples, two brands taken all the way through

Two fictional D2C brands, each run through all ten teammates. Same method, deliberately different shapes, so you can see it hold.

Open the one closest to your category first. Then open the other, because the contrast is where the method becomes visible.

| | [Little Lab](little-lab/) | [Gur & Ghee](gur-and-ghee/) |
|---|---|---|
| Category | Baby skincare | Jaggery sweets and snacks |
| Compliance | CDSCO, cosmetics | FSSAI, food |
| Distribution | Amazon and Flipkart heavy | Own site, Zomato and Swiggy, corporate gifting |
| Positioning | Paediatrician trust, ingredient transparency | Three ingredients, never discounted, honest about sugar |
| Shape of the problem | A trust category with a nervous buyer | A perishable gift with a summer problem |
| Scale | About ₹4.2Cr a year | About ₹22L a month, ₹64L in Diwali month |

**Both brands, their founders, their competitors and every number are invented.** Do not quote either in a real deck. The compliance rules shown are illustrative and are not regulatory advice.

---

## How to use these

**When a session's instructions are clear but you cannot picture the output.** Open the matching folder in the brand closest to yours. Every session file links to the right one.

**When you are running behind.** Copy the relevant subfolder into your own `my-work/` and carry on. You lose personalisation for that one teammate, but the next session still has something to read. Re-run it on your own brand at home.

**When you did no pre-work and have nothing to paste.** See [`catch-up.md`](../catch-up.md). It shows how to borrow Little Lab's inputs for the day.

## What is in each brand

```
<brand>/
├── CLAUDE.md              the filled brand profile, the output of Session 01
├── brand-brain/           the inputs that produced it
│   ├── positioning.md     competitors.md     products.csv
│   ├── reviews.md         unit-economics.md  nominated-skus.md
│   ├── ads/               past ad copy with performance notes
│   └── voice-dna/         the founder's own writing, the highest value folder
├── sample-inputs/         raw exports: orders, ad spend, reviews
└── my-work/               what the ten teammates produced
```

**Read `brand-brain/` before `my-work/`.** The interesting question is what the teammates were given. Compare `brand-brain/voice-dna/` with anything in `my-work/content-lead/` and you can see the voice transfer.

## Gur & Ghee has two dated runs

`gur-and-ghee/my-work/` holds work from **two dates**, 13 June 2026 and 10 October 2026.

- The **June** run is the brand's bootcamp day. Thirty-two pasted reviews and four exported files.
- The **October** run is four months later, with the store and ad accounts connected.

Three teammates have both: the Competitive Analyst, the Customer Researcher and the Growth Analyst. Read the two customer voice reports side by side. Three of June's complaints have nearly disappeared by October, and each one traces to a change the founder made in the week after the bootcamp.

**That is what compounding looks like**, and it is the argument for running these every week.
