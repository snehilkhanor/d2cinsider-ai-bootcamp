# Resources, what to add after the bootcamp

The bootcamp gives you ten teammates working from files. Once you are home, the question becomes what to connect next. This page is the short list, built around how an Indian D2C brand actually operates: WhatsApp, marketplaces, shipping, payments, accounting.

**The rule:** add a tool only when a teammate visibly cannot do its job without it. If it does not feed a teammate or a Monday decision, it does not belong in your stack yet.

**Before you rely on anything below, check it.** Which tools have a ready-made connector changes month to month. The list in the app is the truth, this page is a guide.

---

## How connecting works

In the desktop app, click **+** next to the prompt box and choose **Connectors**. Anything in that list has a built-in sign-in: click, sign in, done. The step-by-step for the first three is in [`after-the-bootcamp/connectors.md`](after-the-bootcamp/connectors.md).

If a tool is not in that list, you have two choices, and neither needs you to write code:

1. **Export a file.** Almost every tool can export a CSV. Save it in `brand-brain/` once a week. Every teammate reads files.
2. **Ask a developer.** A custom connection is a few hours of work for someone technical. It is not a founder job.

**Verify every connection before you trust a number.** Ask "which account am I connected to?" and check that the answer names your business. A confident report about somebody else's store is the most expensive mistake in this whole system.

## Start with these three

| Connect | Helps | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Shopify** | Growth Analyst, Marketplace Manager, Support and Retention Manager, Customer Researcher | Orders, products, customers and stock. The Growth Analyst stops estimating and starts measuring |
| **Google Drive** | Brand Strategist, Content Director, Ad Creative Lead | Brand assets, shared folders, creative handed to an agency |
| **Gmail** | Ops Lead, Customer Researcher | Vendor threads and support emails. Also lets routines email you their summary |

## Then, as your brand needs them

| Tool | Helps | What it adds | How to bring it in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Accounting** (Zoho Books, Tally) | Growth Analyst, Ops Lead | Real costs, GST, returns. Your unit economics stop being a hand-kept file | Connector if listed, otherwise a monthly export |
| **WhatsApp platform** (WATI, AiSensy, Interakt) | Support and Retention Manager, Customer Researcher | Templates, broadcasts, conversation history | Export conversations weekly. Load templates by hand |
| **Payments** (Razorpay and others) | Growth Analyst, Ops Lead | What actually settled, refunds, COD remittance | Weekly settlement export |
| **Shipping** (Shiprocket and others) | Ops Lead, Growth Analyst | Return-to-origin rates, failed deliveries, cost by zone | Weekly export |
| **Email platform** (Klaviyo and others) | Support and Retention Manager | Flow performance, segment sizes | Export, or connector if listed |
| **Reviews app** (Judge.me, Loox and others) | Customer Researcher, Marketplace Manager | The raw material for customer themes | Often arrives through Shopify. Otherwise export |

## Marketplaces

For Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho and quick commerce, plan on files. Download the weekly sales report from the seller portal and save it in `brand-brain/`. The Growth Analyst picks it up. Sample files showing the shape are in `examples/d2c-marketplace-samples/`.

## Usually not worth it yet

| Tool | Why to wait |
|---|---|
| **Meta Ads and Google Ads, connected live** | Setup is fiddly and a weekly export does the job. Revisit when someone on your team manages ads full time |
| **Social media connections** | They report likes and reach. Treat social as somewhere you publish, not a data source |
| **Design tools** | Worth it only if your creative already lives there and someone uses it weekly |

## Teammates worth building next

The bootcamp ships ten. These are the ones founders most often build in the months after, using [`after-the-bootcamp/write-your-own-skill.md`](after-the-bootcamp/write-your-own-skill.md) as the method.

| Teammate | Job | Runs |
|---|---|---|
| **Festival Calendar Planner** | Plans a year of content and campaigns around Indian festivals and your category's own peaks | Once a year, refreshed quarterly |
| **WhatsApp Template Checker** | Checks a draft template against the platform's approval rules before you submit it | Every new template |
| **Label and Listing Compliance Checker** | Checks a listing against the rules for your category before it goes live | Every new product |
| **RTO Predictor** | Flags orders likely to come back before they ship, so someone can call first | Daily, before dispatch |
| **GST and HSN Checker** | Checks codes and rates on invoices | Weekly |
| **Promo Conflict Checker** | Finds discounts that stack by accident and eat margin | Before any new promotion |
| **COD to Prepaid Converter** | Drafts the message that moves a risky cash-on-delivery order to prepaid | Daily |

Build one at a time, starting with the one that would have saved you the most pain last quarter.

## A note on restraint

Founders consistently add too much in the two weeks after a bootcamp. Every new connection is one more thing to break and one more thing for the app to read on every run.

Ten teammates and three connections are enough to run a serious D2C brand. Everything else on this page is something you earn by hitting a wall.
