# D2C Insider AI Bootcamp, Student Handbook

In one day you hire, onboard and put to work ten AI teammates for your brand. Not modules. Not exercises. Teammates with a job description, an inbox, a tool kit and a shipping bar.

By 7 PM every one of them is running on your brand, your data and your competitors, and you have a 90 day plan for what they do next.

This handbook is the map. Each session has its own file. Open one at a time. Every prompt you need is inside the session file.

> **Prefer to browse?** Open [`site/index.html`](site/index.html) in your browser. Same content on one page, plus a glossary and a repo tour. [`site/sessions.html`](site/sessions.html) shows every session's prerequisites, flow and completion check side by side, which is the fastest way to see where you are.

---

## How this runs

Everything happens in the **Claude Code desktop app**, the Mac or Windows application. You will not open a terminal. You install an app, point it at a folder, and type in a chat box.

Three things you will do over and over, so learn them once in Session 0:

1. **Type `/`** to see every teammate available and pick one.
2. **Watch the Tasks pane** when a subagent runs, so you can see work happening rather than waiting at a blank screen.
3. **Click a file path** in the chat to read what your teammate wrote. You never need Finder or Explorer.

## The five ideas behind the ten teammates

You do not need to memorise these. You will use each one during the day.

| Idea | What it is | Where you meet it |
|---|---|---|
| **`CLAUDE.md`** | A permanent profile of your brand, voice, products and customers | Session 01. Every later teammate reads it first |
| **Skills** | Reusable playbooks Claude loads when they are relevant | Sessions 02 and 03 |
| **Subagents** | Teammates that go away, do a big job, and come back with it finished | Sessions 04 and 05 |
| **Routines** | Scheduled runs inside the desktop app | Session 09, the weekly brief |
| **The Chief of Staff pattern** | One subagent that reads across every other teammate | Session 10 |

Connecting live tools such as Shopify, Drive and Gmail is covered in [`after-the-bootcamp/connectors.md`](after-the-bootcamp/connectors.md). During the live day you work from the files in `brand-brain/`, so nobody is stuck waiting on a login.

## Before you arrive

**Bring a Mac or Windows laptop.** The bootcamp cannot be done on a phone, tablet or Chromebook. A phone is handy as a second screen for Zoom.

**Come to the setup session the evening before.** It is on Zoom, the time is in your email, and TAs are there to get your app working and your `brand-brain/` started.

**Fill `templates/brand-brain/`.** Copy that folder to the repo root, rename it `brand-brain`, and put in whatever you have: reviews, competitor notes, product data, your unit economics, and above all samples of your own writing.

Founders who arrive with that folder filled walk out with reports built on real data. Founders who arrive cold spend the morning catching up, and every downstream session inherits the gap.

**Have 15 minutes spare?** Do [`practice-lab.md`](practice-lab.md), a warm-up on a sample brand.

**Did not get to it?** Open [`catch-up.md`](catch-up.md) at 9:00 on the day. It gets you a working `brand-brain/` in ten minutes, or lets you borrow the sample brand.

**Fill what you have and leave the rest.** Session 01 interviews you for anything missing. A gap is a question you answer out loud, not a blocker.

The single highest value folder is `brand-brain/voice-dna/`. Five things you actually wrote. Not written for this, written by you, already sent. That folder is the difference between output that sounds like your brand and output that sounds like every other brand in your category.

## When you cannot picture the output

Two fictional brands are worked all the way through in [`examples/`](examples/README.md): **Little Lab**, baby skincare under CDSCO, sold mostly on marketplaces, and **Gur & Ghee**, jaggery sweets under FSSAI, sold on its own site and quick commerce with a corporate gifting side.

Open whichever is closer to your category. Every session file links to the right folder inside them.

Two things to read in either one. First, `brand-brain/` before `my-work/`, because what a teammate was given is more instructive than what it wrote. Second, the flags. Both examples are full of gaps, refusals and missing licence numbers, left in on purpose. That is what real output looks like.

## Two scopes, every session

Every skill and subagent asks you to pick scope before it starts.

**Default** is sized for a Pro plan and the length of a session. It produces less and teaches the same thing.

**Power** is for a Max plan or for running again at home. More angles, more pieces, more segments.

Pick Default during the live day. Re-run in Power at home when nobody is waiting for you.

## After the bootcamp

Re-run any teammate any week by opening their session file and repeating the steps. They compound. Brand Strategist gets richer, Competitive Analyst keeps tracking, Growth Analyst sharpens as your data grows.

When you want to add more tools, read [`resources.md`](resources.md) before installing anything you saw on a podcast. It lists what is worth adding, in order, and what to skip.

## Stuck

Every session file ends with a troubleshooting section for that session specifically. Beyond that, [`references/session-00-setup/troubleshooting.md`](references/session-00-setup/troubleshooting.md) covers install and repo problems.

On Zoom, use **Raise hand**. A TA pulls you into a breakout room, fixes it with you, and sends you back. The goal is everyone shipping by 7 PM, not everyone finishing alone.
